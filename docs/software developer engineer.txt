About the job
Siemens EDA is a global technology leader in Electronic Design Automation software. Our software tools enable companies around the world to develop highly innovative electronic products faster and more cost-effectively. Our customers use our tools to push the boundaries of technology and physics to deliver better products in the increasingly complex world of chip, board, and system design.

Position Overview

Join our EDA team and work on cutting-edge compiler technologies and optimizations that power the next generation of chip design. Based in Noida, you'll collaborate with global teams to develop innovative software solutions while gaining exposure to diverse projects across India and beyond. This is an excellent opportunity for fresh graduates and early-career engineers to build a strong foundation in software development within a world-class organization. You'll work on real customer problems and grow through mentorship from experienced engineers.

Key Responsibilities

Develop and optimize compiler solutions and tools using C/C++ on UNIX/LINUX platforms
Design and implement efficient algorithms and data structures for EDA applications
Apply compiler concepts and optimization techniques to enhance software performance
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate customer requirements into scalable solutions
Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement of development practices
Test, debug, and refine software components to meet quality standards

Qualifications

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical Engineering (EEE), or Computer Science (CS) from a recognized engineering college
Strong understanding of C/C++ programming, including design patterns, data structures, and algorithms, with hands-on development experience on UNIX/Linux platforms.
Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills
Ability to work effectively with multi-functional teams in a collaborative environment

Good to Have

Knowledge of ML/AI algorithms for data-driven applications
Familiarity with Verilog, SystemVerilog, or VHDL
Understanding of compiler concepts and optimization techniques
Previous exposure to EDA tools or electronic design workflows
